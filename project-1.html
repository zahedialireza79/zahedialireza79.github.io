Mobile Robot Development

Engineering contributions to the design, integration, and build of an industrial mobile robot platform.

Project Overview

This project involved contributing to the development of an industrial mobile robot used in automated material-handling systems. My role covered a broad range of engineering responsibilities across mechanical, electrical, and system-integration domains.

I worked on the robot’s overall hardware architecture — reviewing designs, preparing documentation, and coordinating the mechanical and electrical aspects of the build. I contributed to improving assembly processes, verifying component layouts, and ensuring that the system met reliability and safety requirements.

Throughout the build and validation stages, I supported the engineering team by diagnosing hardware-related issues, refining design decisions, and helping align the robot’s mechanical and electrical subsystems with operational requirements. I also created clear engineering documentation that standardized the assembly workflow.

This project strengthened my experience in robotics hardware development, system-level engineering, and multidisciplinary collaboration—helping me better understand how real-world robotic platforms are designed, built, and validated.
