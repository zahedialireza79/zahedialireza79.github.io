Supporting VLA-based manipulation, imitation-learning workflows, and teleoperation-driven data collection.

Project Overview

This project focused on AI-assisted robotic manipulation using Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and imitation learning. I contributed to the setup, operation, and evaluation of a clothes-folding robotic workflow powered by learning-based methods.

My work included configuring and running the robot platform, performing testing and demonstrations, and supporting structured data collection used for imitation-learning pipelines.

I also worked with ALOHA-style Trossen teleoperation hardware, integrating ROS and LeRobot for teleoperation, data acquisition, and model-driven evaluation.

This experience strengthened my skills in robotics software, teleoperation systems, and modern learning-based manipulation workflows used in research and development environments.
